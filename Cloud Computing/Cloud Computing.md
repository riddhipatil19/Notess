# Definition 
**Cloud Computing** is the delivery of computing services (like servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and AI) **over the Internet (“the cloud”)** instead of owning and maintaining physical computers or servers yourself.
It means **renting IT resources online** on a pay-as-you-go basis instead of buying expensive hardware.
